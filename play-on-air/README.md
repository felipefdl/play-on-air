# PlayOnAir

AirPlay 2 for your Google Cast speakers.

Nest speakers, Chromecasts, and TVs with Chromecast built-in show up in the AirPlay picker on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Play to one, or select several and they play in sync. Add them to the Apple Home app, put them in rooms, and ask Siri to play there.

## Features

- **Feels like a native AirPlay speaker.** Controls act the moment you tap them: pause and switching to another output reach the speaker in about a tenth of a second; skip, seek, and resume start in well under a second. The speaker still keeps 8 s of audio queued, so Wi-Fi hiccups don't interrupt playback.
- **Lossless to the speaker.** AirPlay audio is decoded once and sent to Cast as FLAC (WAV when a device rejects FLAC), never re-encoded to a lossy format.
- **Multi-room in sync.** Speakers selected together play through Google Cast multizone, the same mechanism the Google Home app uses.
- **Apple Home and Siri.** Assign speakers to rooms and play to them by name or by room.
- **Zero setup.** Install and start: every Cast speaker on the network shows up. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.

## Before you install

PlayOnAir runs on the **host network** so mDNS discovery and AirPlay and Cast traffic reach your LAN. The optional **Configuration** tab sets the log level and renames or hides speakers.

The **Documentation** tab covers setup, Apple Home, troubleshooting, and limits.
